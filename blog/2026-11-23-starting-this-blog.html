Starting this blog

November 23, 2026

I’m starting this blog as a place to write in public about AI safety, evaluation, and the research questions I’m chasing — especially reward hacking, probes, and how specialized AI systems stay under human control.

Posts will be short and uneven on purpose. Some will be technical notes. Some will be reflections from building systems in production. The goal is to think clearly in writing, not to polish every paragraph into a paper.

If you’re here for a research conversation: welcome. More soon.
